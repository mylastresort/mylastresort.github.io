# Awards & Achievements Tracker

> Source of truth for awards, honors, and achievements intended for the
> "Awards & Achievements" resume section (`resume/resume.tex`, currently empty).
> Format contract mirrors `src/data/projects.md` — keep entries in this exact shape.
> Status: `validated` (proven, may appear on resumes) or `pending` (evidence not confirmed yet).
> Honesty rules apply (see `AGENTS.md`): never list an award that is not `validated`;
> never inflate titles, ranks, or issuer names. If evidence is thin, say less.
>
> Injection into per-role resumes (`scripts/resumes/generate.mjs`) is not wired up yet —
> it will be added in a later version once the placement and format are approved.
> Rendering rule (per resume-section-builder: "additional sections add value, not filler"):
> the section is emitted only when at least one entry is `validated` — never shipped empty.
